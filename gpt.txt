🥇 MOST OPTIMAL FILE PROFILE (ORDERED)

This is the sweet spot for storage + quality + compatibility.

🥇 1. Video Codec
✅ Prefer:
x265 / HEVC (8-bit or 10-bit)


Why:

~40% smaller than x264

Excellent quality

Modern TVs + Apple TV + most devices support it

Saves massive storage long term

If you have very old TVs → choose x264 instead.

🥈 2. Resolution
Default:
1080p


Best balance of:

Storage

Quality

Direct play

Low NAS stress

Only if you REALLY want 4K:
2160p WEB-DL x265 (12–25GB range)


Avoid massive 60GB+ releases.

🥉 3. Container
MKV


Most flexible and widely supported.

MP4 is also fine.

🥉 4. Audio
Best:
AAC
AC3 (Dolby Digital)


These almost never trigger transcoding.

🥉 5. Release Type
Prefer:
WEB-DL
WEBRip
BluRay x265 re-encode


Why:

Clean source

Good compression

Reasonable size

🧊 Ideal File Size Targets
Type	Ideal Size
1080p x265	4–10 GB
1080p x264	6–12 GB
4K x265	12–25 GB

This gives best balance long-term.

❌ ABSOLUTELY AVOID (IN ORDER)
🚫 1. REMUX Releases
REMUX
BluRay REMUX
Full BluRay
BDISO


Why avoid:

50–80GB per movie

Waste massive storage

Often forces audio transcoding

No visible difference on most TVs unless high-end setup

🚫 2. AV1 (For Now)
AV1


Why:

Not widely supported on TVs

Will force transcoding

Your NAS will struggle

🚫 3. DTS-HD / TrueHD / Atmos (Unless Needed)

These often cause:

Audio transcoding

CPU usage spikes

If your TV/receiver supports passthrough → fine.
If not → avoid.

🚫 4. 60+ GB 4K Files

Unless:

You have 40TB+ storage

All devices direct play HEVC + HDR

You truly want max quality

Otherwise → wasteful.
